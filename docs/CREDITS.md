# OpenZ credits

**OpenZ** is a faithful fan tribute remake of Z by The Bitmap Brothers (1996), celebrating its 30th anniversary.
**30th anniversary · open-source 3D remake** (Z 1996-2026). Four levels, rebuilt in 3D. Open source (GPL-3.0, see
LICENSE), free and not for sale: a non-commercial fan showcase.

## Original materials only
- OpenZ uses no copyrighted material from the original Z: no Bitmap Brothers sprites, sounds, music, voice recordings,
  art or logos. Short in-game phrases such as "Robot manufactured" are spoken in our own new recordings.
- Every model, texture, sound, voice, music track and UI element is newly made: assets from our earlier projects and
  procedural generation.
- The gameplay rules come from the open-source Zod Engine (GPLv3).
- Z © Rebellion. Originally created by The Bitmap Brothers (1996). OpenZ is an unofficial, free, non-commercial fan
  remake, not affiliated with or endorsed by Rebellion. Z, its names and its characters belong to their owners.

## Original game
- **Z** (1996) by **The Bitmap Brothers**: the game design, levels, units and rules that OpenZ pays tribute to.

## Rules, maps and CPU opponent
- **Zod Engine** by **Michael Bok / Nighsoft**, GPLv3. OpenZ ports its gameplay rules, unit parameters, production and
  territory logic, pathfinding behaviour and the CPU bot (`zbot.cpp`) to C#; the level 2, level 7, level 16 and level 17 map data are
  decoded from the Zod Engine map files. Because of this port, OpenZ is licensed GPL-3.0.
